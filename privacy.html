Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-25

This policy covers the taBtaP iPhone app and Mac Agent. The developer doesn't run any servers, and the apps don't send personal information to the developer. Ads in the iPhone app are provided by Google AdMob, and the information in section 3 is handled by Google.

1. Between your iPhone and Mac

The iPhone app and Mac Agent connect directly on your local network, encrypted with a key created when you pair. The iPhone sends only the number of the button you pressed and slider values; the Mac sends back the button layout and results. This information never leaves the two devices.

2. Stored on your devices

Mac: button layout, paired iPhones (device name and pairing key), and Agent settings — in ~/Library/Application Support/TabTap.
iPhone: your paired Mac and its pairing key (Keychain), the button layout received from the Mac, appearance setting, when an ad was last shown, and whether you bought Remove Ads.
Deleting the app or the folder above deletes this information.

3. Ads (iPhone app)

The iPhone app shows banner and full-screen ads with Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier, if you allow it), approximate location inferred from your IP address, ad impression and tap data, and app usage and diagnostic data. Tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it in iOS's Allow Tracking prompt. In the EEA and similar regions you can choose in a consent form and change it later from the app's ⋯ menu. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled by the Apple App Store. The developer doesn't receive payment information; the app only checks on your device whether you bought it.

5. Permissions

Local Network: only to find and connect to your Mac on the same Wi-Fi.
Camera (iPhone): only to read the pairing QR code. No photos or video are saved or sent.
Accessibility (Mac): only to read and switch windows and press the keys you set on a button. Your screen and typing are not recorded.
Tracking (iPhone): only for personalized ads. The app works the same if you don't allow it.

6. Children

taBtaP is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
